What is a Logistic Company?

As captured in our core philosophy, a logistic company is a business that specializes in planning, implementing, and managing the efficient movement and storage of goods, services, or information within a supply chain. At AS BERJAYA, we handle various aspects of transportation, warehousing, inventory management, and distribution, ensuring that products are delivered from the point of origin to the final destination in a timely and cost-effective manner.
