What is a Logistic Company?

A logistic company is a business that specializes in planning, implementing, and managing the efficient movement and storage of goods, services, or information within a supply chain. They handle various aspects of transportation, warehousing, inventory management, and distribution, ensuring that products are delivered from the point of origin to the final destination in a timely and cost-effective manner.
